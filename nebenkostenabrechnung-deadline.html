Nebenkostenabrechnung Deadline: When Must Your Landlord Send It?

If you rent in Germany and pay monthly advance payments for utilities, your landlord has to settle them once a year in a Nebenkostenabrechnung. The law sets a deadline for that statement, the Abrechnungsfrist. For the usual calendar-year statement for 2025, it ends on 31 December 2026. Here is what the rule says, what counts as "received", and what a late statement changes. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Short answer: The landlord has to get the statement to the tenant by the end of the 12th month after the billing period ends (§ 556 Abs. 3 BGB). For calendar-year 2025, that is 31 December 2026. If it arrives later, the landlord generally can no longer demand an extra payment, unless the delay was not their responsibility. A refund owed to the tenant does not expire.

The 12-month rule in plain English

With advance payments, the landlord has to settle the utility costs every year. The statute (§ 556 Abs. 3 BGB) says the statement must be communicated to the tenant at the latest by the end of the twelfth month after the end of the billing period (the Abrechnungszeitraum). It is a cut-off date (Ausschlussfrist), not a recommendation.

The billing period is normally the calendar year, but it can be another 12-month period if your lease or earlier statements use one. It cannot be longer than 12 months. So the first thing to look at on any statement is the period it covers, because the deadline is counted from the end of that period.

Deadline by billing period

Billing period Must reach the tenant by Status on 19 Nov 2026
1 Jan – 31 Dec 2025 31 Dec 2026 Open (the most common case)
1 Apr 2025 – 31 Mar 2026 31 Mar 2027 Open
1 Oct 2024 – 30 Sep 2025 30 Sep 2026 Passed
1 Jul 2024 – 30 Jun 2025 30 Jun 2026 Passed
1 Jan – 31 Dec 2024 31 Dec 2025 Passed

The same arithmetic works for any other period: take the last day of the billing period and add 12 months.

"Received" means received

The deadline is about when the statement reaches the tenant, not when the landlord writes or posts it. A letter dated 30 December that lands in the mailbox in January is late. Tenants' associations such as the Berliner Mieterverein put the risk of a late delivery on the landlord, and a landlord who relies on the deadline generally has to be able to show when the statement arrived.

For a letter in a mailbox, "received" generally means it was dropped in at a time when the tenant could normally be expected to collect it. The tenant does not have to have opened it. A letter put in the mailbox late on New Year's Eve may therefore not count as received that day. This is why tenants often keep the envelope and note the date they found the letter.

What if you moved out during the year?

Moving out does not start the clock or shorten it. The 12 months still run from the end of the billing period, and a landlord is not required to issue a separate statement when a tenant leaves in the middle of a year. If you left in March 2025 and your lease bills by calendar year, a statement for 2025 can still arrive in time until 31 December 2026. It will cover only the months you lived there.

What a late statement means

If the statement reaches the tenant after the deadline, the landlord's right to claim an extra payment (Nachforderung) is generally excluded. The exception is where the landlord is not responsible for the delay (§ 556 Abs. 3 Satz 3 BGB). The landlord has to explain concretely why it happened and prove it. A vague reference to "organisational reasons" is not the kind of explanation courts look for.

Tenant advice sources mention situations such as a property-tax assessment that was only set after the deadline, or a supplier's invoice that arrived too late. Whether an exception applies depends on the facts of each case.

Owner-occupied flats follow the same rule. In a decision of 25 January 2017 (VIII ZR 249/15), the BGH held that a landlord of a condominium flat is bound by the deadline too, and has to show their own efforts to get the owners' association figures in time.

What the deadline does not do is wipe out amounts owed to the tenant. If a late statement shows a credit (Guthaben), it stays payable. Late or not, a statement that was delivered in time can also still contain mistakes: the deadline says nothing about whether the numbers are right.

Two separate 12-month clocks

There are two different 12-month periods, and they are easy to mix up.

Landlord's clock Tenant's clock
Starts When the billing period ends When the tenant receives the statement
Ends The statement must have reached the tenant Objections must have reached the landlord
If missed Extra payment generally can't be claimed (unless the delay was not the landlord's responsibility) Objections generally can't be raised any more (unless the delay was not the tenant's responsibility)
Statute § 556 Abs. 3 Sätze 2–3 BGB § 556 Abs. 3 Sätze 5–6 BGB

The tenant's clock is about objections (Einwendungen), such as a cost that does not belong in the statement. It applies to a formally proper statement, and it is not a payment deadline. An objection does not automatically push back the due date of a payment demanded in the statement. The statute speaks of "communicating" objections to the landlord. Consumer guidance generally recommends doing it in writing so there is proof of the date. For how to read the statement itself, see the main Nebenkostenabrechnung guide.

If no statement arrives

A tenant who pays advance payments is entitled to an annual statement, and tenant advice sources note that it can be requested from the landlord, and if necessary claimed in court. Where the deadline has passed without a statement, a credit is still possible, so many tenants' associations suggest asking for one rather than letting the matter drop. What fits a specific case, for example when the tenancy has already ended, is something a Mieterverein or a lawyer can look at.

When the rule works differently

  • Flat-rate utilities: if your lease includes utilities in a flat monthly amount instead of advance payments, there is generally no annual statement to settle.
  • Commercial leases: the rule discussed here is for residential tenancies. Commercial leases are handled differently.
  • Heating costs: heating and hot water are usually part of the same settlement and fall under the same deadline. They are billed under their own rules (Heizkostenverordnung).

Dates worth writing down

  • The billing period shown on the statement, and whether your lease uses the calendar year.
  • The day the statement reached you, and the envelope if it came by post.
  • The date any objection reaches the landlord.

Frequently asked questions

Until when can my landlord send the Nebenkostenabrechnung for 2025?

If your billing period is the calendar year 1 January to 31 December 2025, the statement has to reach you by the end of 31 December 2026. The rule is that the landlord must communicate the statement no later than the end of the twelfth month after the billing period ends (§ 556 Abs. 3 BGB). A statement for a different 12-month period, such as 1 July to 30 June, has a correspondingly different date.

Does the date on the letter or the postmark count?

No. What matters is when the statement reaches the tenant, not when it was written or posted. Tenants' associations such as the Berliner Mieterverein explain that the risk of a late delivery lies with the landlord. In a dispute, the landlord generally has to show that the statement arrived in time.

My statement arrived after the deadline. Do I still have to pay the extra amount?

As a general rule, no. After the deadline the landlord can no longer claim an additional payment (Nachforderung), unless the delay was not their responsibility. Typical examples in tenant advice are a property-tax notice or a utility company's bill that only arrived after the deadline, but the landlord has to explain and prove the reason. Whether an exception applies in a particular case is a question for a Mieterverein or a lawyer.

Do I lose a refund (Guthaben) if the statement is late?

No. The deadline protects tenants from late demands for extra payments. A credit shown in the statement does not expire because the statement is late.

I moved out in spring 2025. When is the deadline for me?

Moving out does not shorten the landlord's time. The 12 months run from the end of the billing period, and landlords are not required to issue a separate statement when a tenant leaves mid-year. With calendar-year billing, a tenant who left in March 2025 can still receive a timely statement for 2025 until 31 December 2026.

How long do I have to object to a statement?

Tenants have their own, separate 12 months: objections must reach the landlord by the end of the twelfth month after the statement was received (§ 556 Abs. 3 Satz 5 BGB), and after that they are generally excluded. This is not a payment deadline. Objecting does not automatically postpone the date on which a payment demanded in the statement is due.

Does the deadline apply if I rent a flat inside a condominium (WEG)?

Yes. The Federal Court of Justice (BGH) has held that a landlord of an owner-occupied flat is bound by the same deadline, and that waiting for the owners' association's Hausgeldabrechnung does not by itself excuse a late statement (BGH, 25 January 2017, VIII ZR 249/15). The landlord has to show their own efforts to get the figures in time.

Is there a statement at all if I pay a flat rate for utilities?

If your lease says the utilities are included in a flat monthly amount (Pauschale or Inklusivmiete) rather than advance payments, there is generally no annual statement to settle. Commercial leases are not covered by the residential rule in the same way. Your lease wording decides which applies.
