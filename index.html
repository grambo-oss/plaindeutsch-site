FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Short, honest answers about what PlainDeutsch does and doesn't do.

What does PlainDeutsch do?

PlainDeutsch explains German documents in plain English. Paste the text or upload a PDF of your Mietvertrag (lease) or Nebenkostenabrechnung (annual utility statement) and an explanation of the document arrives by email, usually within minutes.

Is this legal advice?

No. PlainDeutsch explains what a document says in plain language. It does not provide Rechtsberatung, does not tell you what action to take, and is not a substitute for a Rechtsanwalt. For a dispute or a binding decision, speak to a licensed lawyer or a tenants' association such as your local Mieterverein.

How much does it cost?

€5 per Nebenkostenabrechnung and €8 per Mietvertrag, paid once through Gumroad. There is no subscription and no PlainDeutsch account. No VAT is charged (Kleinunternehmerregelung, § 19 UStG).

Which documents does it cover?

Today: German lease agreements (Mietvertrag) and annual utility statements (Nebenkostenabrechnung), as pasted text or a PDF. Seven more tools, including an employment contract explainer and a tax FAQ, are planned.

Can the explanation be wrong?

Yes. The explanations are generated by AI using instructions written for these two document types and German tenancy and utility-billing rules, but they can still be wrong or miss context. Treat the result as a starting point and check anything important with your Mieterverein or a Rechtsanwalt.

How is my document handled?

Your text or file is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the explanation, and the result is emailed to you. Our privacy policy lists every service involved and how long data is kept.

Why not just paste my document into a general chatbot?

You can, and it may help. PlainDeutsch is for people who would rather not write their own prompt: each document type gets the same structure (summary, line-by-line breakdown, key numbers, what to check, next steps) and is written with German rules in mind. It is still AI output, so the same caution applies.

Legal notice: PlainDeutsch explains documents in plain language. It does not provide legal advice (Rechtsberatung) and is not a substitute for a qualified lawyer (Rechtsanwalt). If you are in a legal dispute or need binding legal guidance, please consult a licensed legal professional. PlainDeutsch operates within the limits of the Rechtsdienstleistungsgesetz (RDG).
